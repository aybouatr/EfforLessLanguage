How It Works

A simple five-step flow

1

Sign up

Set up your profile once — name, nationality, and basic information.

Select your interests once — movies, sports, travel, business, music, cooking, gaming, and more.

We use your profile and interests to help you find people with things in common.

2

Match

The app matches you with another user who is currently online and available for a conversation.

Both users must have at least one shared interest, and the app considers their remaining conversation time when creating a match.

If multiple users are available, the app prioritizes the best match based on shared interests, availability, and remaining time.

Users are shown only when they are available to start a conversation, so you don't get matched with someone whose session is about to end.

3

Conversation

A 1:1 face-to-face session for 7 or 10 minutes with shared topics, live chat, Topic Gifts, and simple language games to keep the conversation fun and engaging.

4

AI feedback

After the call, AI flags mispronounced words, gives a quick grammar note, and logs how you rated your partner — enjoyed it, match again, or not for me.

The Product

Three features, one reinforcing loop

Each session feeds the Dashboard. The Dashboard feeds the Leaderboard. Both pull people back into the next session.

01  ·  THE CORE LOOP

1:1 Session

  • Trigger: user taps "Find a match"
  • Matching logic: shared interest + online availability + remaining session time
  • Session: 7 or 10 min live call, with Topic Gifts and language mini-games
  • Post-session: AI feedback on pronunciation and grammar, plus a rating — enjoyed / match again / not for me
  • Feeds: saved buddies, and the data behind the Dashboard and Leaderboard
02  ·  THE SOCIAL HOOK

Leaderboard

  • Ranking metrics: most conversations this week, longest active streak, most "match again" ratings received
  • Scope: resets weekly, so newcomers always have a fresh shot at ranking
  • Segments: split by interest category, e.g. "Top in Travel talkers"
  • Reward: a badge or title on your profile — status, not a prize
03  ·  THE PROGRESS HUB

Dashboard

  • Streak tracker: consecutive days with a session
  • Stats: total conversations, minutes talked, interests practiced
  • AI recap: a weekly digest of mispronounced words and grammar patterns to work on
  • Saved buddies: who you've connected with, and who's online now
  • Levels: visible progress, e.g. "Level 4 Conversationalist"

Session → Dashboard shows personal growth → Leaderboard shows how you compare → both bring you back to the next Session.
